Getting Started

KnowledgeHub is a community blogging platform for passionate people who love sharing knowledge. Whether you're into design, tech, fitness, cooking, or anything else — if you love learning and sharing, you belong here. It's free to join and start writing immediately.
Yes! The core platform is completely free. You can write unlimited posts, build your public profile, join community groups, and connect with readers — all for free. We also offer a Pro plan (₱299/month) and Team plan (₱999/month) for creators who want advanced features like custom domains, analytics, and monetization tools.
Simply create a free account, verify your email, and you'll have access to our editor right away. Click "New Post" from your dashboard, write your content, add a cover image, choose a topic tag, and hit Publish. Your post goes live instantly to the community.

Account & Profile

Absolutely! Your author profile includes a bio, profile photo, banner image, social media links, and a showcase of all your published posts. Pro users can also set a custom username URL (e.g., knowledgehub.com/@yourname).
Visit any writer's profile and click the "Follow" button. You'll get notified whenever they publish a new post. You can manage who you follow from your personal dashboard under the "Following" tab.
Yes, you have full control over your content. From your dashboard, go to "My Posts," click the three-dot menu on any post, and choose Edit or Delete. Deleted posts are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Community & Safety

Almost anything you're passionate about! Design, technology, self-improvement, education, travel, fitness, cooking, music, photography, gaming, finance — if there's knowledge to share, it belongs here. The only rule is that content must be respectful, original, and not harmful.
We have a community moderation team and automated tools that review flagged content. All users can report posts or comments that violate our community guidelines. We take reports seriously and respond within 24 hours. Repeat offenders can be suspended or banned.
Yes! You can join as many groups as you like. Groups are topic-based communities where members share posts, have discussions, and organize events around a shared interest. You can also create your own group once you've published at least 3 posts.

Pro & Billing

Go to your account settings and click "Upgrade to Pro." We accept GCash, PayMaya, credit/debit cards, and bank transfer. Your Pro features activate immediately after payment.
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your billing settings with no penalties. You'll continue to have Pro access until the end of your current billing period, then automatically revert to the free plan.
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new Pro subscribers. If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days, contact our support team and we'll issue a full refund, no questions asked.
